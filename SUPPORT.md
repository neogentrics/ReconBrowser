# Getting help

**Something is wrong.** [Report a bug](https://github.com/neogentrics/ReconBrowser/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
Include your version from *Help → About Recon Browser* and the steps that cause it. A screenshot helps.

**You have an idea.** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/neogentrics/ReconBrowser/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
Check the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) first, since it may already be planned.

**It is a security problem.** Please do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Support is on a best-effort basis.
